# Getting started guides

> PassCollect guides about getting started.

Canonical: https://lucadevelops.com/blog/tags/getting-started/

- [How to open a loyalty card with one tap from your Lock Screen or Home Screen](https://lucadevelops.com/blog/lock-screen-shortcuts-home-screen-widgets/index.md): Set up PassCollect Lock Screen shortcuts and Home Screen widgets to open your most-used loyalty cards with a single tap on iPhone.
- [How to add a loyalty card to PassCollect by scanning its barcode](https://lucadevelops.com/blog/add-loyalty-card-scan-barcode/index.md): Step-by-step guide to adding a loyalty card in PassCollect by scanning the barcode or uploading a photo, then setting its name, logo, barcode type, color and images.
- [How do you store loyalty cards on iPhone without creating an account?](https://lucadevelops.com/blog/store-loyalty-cards-iphone-without-account/index.md): Use PassCollect to keep loyalty cards, passes and memberships on your iPhone with no sign-up or login. Your cards stay on your device and in your own iCloud.
