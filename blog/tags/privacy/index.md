# Privacy guides

> PassCollect guides about privacy.

Canonical: https://lucadevelops.com/blog/tags/privacy/

- [How do you store loyalty cards on iPhone without creating an account?](https://lucadevelops.com/blog/store-loyalty-cards-iphone-without-account/index.md): Use PassCollect to keep loyalty cards, passes and memberships on your iPhone with no sign-up or login. Your cards stay on your device and in your own iCloud.
- [Where is my card data stored? PassCollect's privacy model explained](https://lucadevelops.com/blog/passcollect-privacy-where-card-data-is-stored/index.md): PassCollect doesn't collect, store or share your data. Cards live on your iPhone and are backed up through your own iCloud account, with no servers, analytics or third parties involved.
