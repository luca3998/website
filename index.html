FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PassCollect

Do I need to create an account to use PassCollect?

No account or login is required. All your cards and passes are stored securely on your device and synced privately through your own iCloud account.

How do I add a card to PassCollect?

Scan a barcode with your camera or upload a photo of your card, and PassCollect instantly saves a digital copy you can access anytime.

Does PassCollect work with Apple Watch?

Yes. PassCollect includes full Apple Watch support, so you can access your cards directly from your wrist.

Does PassCollect support iPhone Duo?

Yes. PassCollect's layout adapts to iPhone Duo, so your cards fit the screen whether the phone is folded or unfolded.

Is my card data private and secure?

Yes. PassCollect never uploads your data to our servers. Everything is backed up privately through iCloud, and only you have access to it.

Can I access my cards from the Lock Screen or Home Screen?

Yes. PassCollect offers Lock Screen shortcuts and Home Screen widgets so you can get to your most-used cards with a single tap.
