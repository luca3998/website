# PassCollect Privacy Policy

Canonical: https://lucadevelops.com/privacy/

**Effective Date:** December 2024

We value your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle
your information when you use our app.

## Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or share any personal or non-personal information from users of our app.

## Third-Party Services

Our app does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics tools, or external APIs that
collect user data.

## Permissions

Our app may request certain permissions (e.g., access to the camera, microphone, or local storage)
to provide core functionality. These permissions are used solely within the app and no data is
transmitted or stored outside of your device.

## Children's Privacy

Our app is safe for users of all ages and does not collect any data, including from children under
the age of 13.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected here, and the
updated date will be indicated at the top of this policy.
